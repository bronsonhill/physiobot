## Getting Started

Welcome to the enhanced PhysioBot platform! This new version features real-time audio conversations that provide a more realistic clinical experience. You'll now speak directly with AI patients and supervisors instead of typing.

### What's New in Phase 5

- **🎤 Real-time Audio Conversations**: Speak naturally with AI patients
- **📊 Enhanced Interface**: Modern, intuitive design with visual feedback
- **⚙️ Audio Controls**: Volume, quality, and recording management
- **📝 Live Transcripts**: See your conversation in real-time
- **🎵 Audio Visualization**: Visual feedback for audio activity
- **🔧 Troubleshooting Tools**: Built-in diagnostic features

### System Overview

The PhysioBot experience consists of two main conversations:
1. **Patient Conversation**: Conduct your clinical assessment
2. **Supervisor Conversation**: Receive feedback and guidance

## Audio Setup

### Initial Setup

1. **Browser Requirements**
   - Use Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest versions)
   - Ensure JavaScript is enabled
   - Allow microphone permissions when prompted

2. **Microphone Test**
   - Click "Test Microphone" if available
   - Speak clearly - you should see volume indicators respond
   - Adjust your microphone position for optimal clarity

3. **Audio Quality**
   - Use a quiet environment
   - Consider using a headset for better audio quality
   - Ensure stable internet connection

### Microphone Permissions

When you first access the audio interface:

1. Your browser will ask for microphone permission
2. Click **"Allow"** or **"Grant Permission"**
3. If denied, click the microphone icon in your browser's address bar
4. Select "Allow" and refresh the page

### Audio Settings

- **Volume Control**: Adjust output volume using the slider
- **Quality Indicators**: Monitor connection quality in real-time
- **Fallback Options**: Text input available if audio fails

## Patient Conversation

### Starting Your Conversation

1. **Navigate to Patient Conversation**
   - Enter your student identifier on the Home page
   - Click on "Patient Conversation" in the sidebar

2. **Initialize Audio**
   - Click **"🎙️ Start Audio Conversation"**
   - Wait for the connection indicator to show "🟢 Ready for your input"

3. **Audio Status Indicators**
   - 🔵 **Ready to start**: System is initializing
   - 🟡 **Connecting**: Establishing audio connection
   - 🟢 **Ready for input**: You can start speaking
   - 🔴 **Recording**: Your voice is being captured
   - 🎵 **AI responding**: Patient is speaking
   - ✅ **Completed**: Conversation finished

### During the Conversation

#### Recording Your Voice

1. **Start Recording**
   - Click **"🔴 Start Recording"** when ready to speak
   - The button will change to show recording status
   - Speak clearly and naturally

2. **Stop Recording**
   - Click **"⏹️ Stop Recording"** when you finish speaking
   - Wait for processing (🟡 Processing indicator)
   - Listen to the patient's audio response

#### Interface Elements

- **Volume Indicator**: Shows your microphone input level
- **Live Transcript**: Displays conversation in real-time
- **Duration Timer**: Tracks conversation length
- **Audio Visualization**: Visual representation of audio activity

#### Text Backup Option

If audio isn't working:
1. Use the **"💬 Text Input"** section
2. Type your message in the text box
3. Click **"Send Text"** to continue

### Best Practices for Patient Interviews

1. **Speak Naturally**
   - Use conversational tone
   - Ask open-ended questions
   - Allow natural pauses

2. **Listen Actively**
   - Wait for complete responses
   - Don't interrupt the AI patient
   - Pay attention to verbal cues

3. **Follow Clinical Process**
   - Use WOCCSNOR assessment framework
   - Ask about symptoms systematically
   - Gather comprehensive history

4. **Monitor Audio Quality**
   - Watch volume indicators
   - Ensure clear speech
   - Restate if asked to clarify

### Ending the Conversation

1. **Finish Button**
   - Click **"Finish Conversation"** when assessment is complete
   - Conversation will be automatically saved
   - You'll be able to proceed to supervisor conversation

2. **Automatic Limits**
   - Conversations have maximum duration limits
   - System will warn you as you approach limits
   - Save regularly during long conversations

## Supervisor Conversation

### Overview

After completing your patient conversation, you'll discuss your assessment with an AI supervisor who provides feedback and guidance.

### Features

- **Contextual Feedback**: Supervisor has access to your patient conversation
- **Educational Guidance**: Receive coaching on clinical skills
- **Assessment Review**: Discuss your diagnostic approach
- **Skill Development**: Learn from areas for improvement

### Process

1. **Access Supervisor Page**
   - Complete patient conversation first
   - Navigate to "Supervisor Conversation"

2. **Audio or Text Mode**
   - Enhanced audio interface (similar to patient conversation)
   - Or traditional text-based interaction

3. **Discussion Topics**
   - Review your assessment approach
   - Discuss diagnostic considerations
   - Receive feedback on communication skills
   - Plan treatment strategies

## Troubleshooting

### Common Audio Issues

#### No Audio Input
**Problem**: Microphone not working
**Solutions**:
- Check browser permissions
- Verify microphone is connected
- Try refreshing the page
- Use text input as backup

#### Poor Audio Quality
**Problem**: Distorted or unclear audio
**Solutions**:
- Move to quieter environment
- Check internet connection
- Adjust microphone position
- Consider using headset

#### Connection Issues
**Problem**: Frequent disconnections
**Solutions**:
- Check internet stability
- Close other bandwidth-heavy applications
- Try different browser
- Contact technical support

### Browser-Specific Issues

#### Chrome
- Ensure microphone permissions are granted
- Check site settings for audio permissions
- Clear browser cache if needed

#### Firefox
- Allow audio autoplay in settings
- Check privacy settings for microphone access
- Disable tracking protection if necessary

#### Safari
- Enable camera and microphone in settings
- Check website-specific permissions
- Update to latest version

### Technical Diagnostics

#### Built-in Tests
Use the diagnostic tools in the admin interface:
- **Microphone Test**: Verify audio input
- **Playback Test**: Check audio output
- **Connection Test**: Validate network connectivity
- **System Health**: Overall system status

#### Manual Checks
1. **Browser Console**: Look for error messages
2. **Network Tab**: Check for failed requests
3. **Permissions**: Verify all required permissions granted
4. **Updates**: Ensure browser is current

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Can I use this on mobile devices?**
A: The system is optimized for desktop/laptop use. Mobile compatibility may vary.

**Q: What if my internet connection is unstable?**
A: Use the text input backup option, or wait for a more stable connection.

**Q: How long are conversations saved?**
A: Transcripts are stored according to your institution's data retention policy.

**Q: Can I pause during a conversation?**
A: Yes, you can pause between exchanges. Avoid pausing during AI responses.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why isn't my microphone working?**
A: Check browser permissions, ensure microphone is connected, and verify system audio settings.

**Q: The audio quality is poor. What can I do?**
A: Try using a headset, moving to a quieter location, or checking your internet connection.

**Q: Can I adjust the AI voice speed or volume?**
A: Volume can be adjusted using the interface controls. Voice settings may be configurable by your instructor.

### Educational Questions

**Q: How does this compare to text-based conversations?**
A: Audio conversations provide more realistic clinical experience and help develop verbal communication skills.

**Q: Will my conversations be reviewed by instructors?**
A: Transcripts may be available to instructors for educational purposes, depending on your program.

**Q: What if I make mistakes during the conversation?**
A: This is a learning environment. Use mistakes as learning opportunities and discuss them with supervisors.

## Appendix

### Keyboard Shortcuts
- **Spacebar**: Quick record (when available)
- **Escape**: Stop recording
- **Tab**: Navigate between controls

### Audio Quality Guidelines
- **Excellent (4.5-5.0)**: Crystal clear, no background noise
- **Good (3.5-4.4)**: Clear with minimal background noise
- **Fair (2.5-3.4)**: Understandable with some interference
- **Poor (Below 2.5)**: Difficult to understand, consider text mode

### Status Code Reference
- **200**: Connected and ready
- **400**: Client error (check permissions)
- **500**: Server error (contact support)
- **Timeout**: Connection lost (check internet)
